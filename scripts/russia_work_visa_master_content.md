## 2. The 3 Official Legal Categories of Russian Work Visas

Foreign nationals entering the Russian Federation for salaried corporate employment must hold an **Ordinary Work Visa (Обыкновенная рабочая виза - Code 003)**. Under **Federal Law No. 115-FZ**, there are three distinct statutory tracks under which a Russian work visa and work permit (*Razreshenie na rabotu*) are issued:

### Category 1: Standard Quota Work Visa (Обыкновенная рабочая виза по квоте)
The vast majority of skilled tradesmen, factory operators, civil construction personnel, and warehouse staff deploy under the **Annual Regional Labour Quota System**:
- **Mechanism:** Russian companies submit detailed manpower requirements to their regional Interdepartmental Migration Commission (*Mezhvedomstvennaya komissiya*) 12 months in advance. Once approved by Mintrud, the employer is allocated a fixed quota (*Kvota*) to hire foreign workers from non-CIS visa-regime countries (such as India).
- **Visa Validity & Extension:** Issued initially as a **Single-Entry 90-Day Work Visa** by the Russian Embassy or VFS center in India. Upon arrival in Russia and registration with the **[Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD)](https://мвд.рф)**, the visa is converted into a **1-Year Multi-Entry Work Visa** stamped directly into the candidate's passport.
- **Renewability:** The 1-year work visa can be renewed annually inside Russia by the employer without the worker having to exit the country, provided the employment contract remains active.
- **Territorial Restriction:** The worker is legally authorized to work **only** within the administrative territory (federal subject) specified on their plastic Work Permit card (e.g., Moscow City, Moscow Oblast, Saint Petersburg, or Tatarstan).

### Category 2: Non-Quota Work Visa (Внеквотные специальности)
To attract specialized technicians and prevent project delays in critical engineering and culinary trades, the Russian Ministry of Labour publishes an official annual **Directory of Non-Quota Professions (*Perechen vnekvotnykh professiy*)**:
- **Eligibility:** Applicable to certified engineering disciplines (civil, electrical, mechanical), specialized high-pressure boiler welders, turners, computer-numerical-control (CNC) machine programmers, head chefs, and executive project directors.
- **Advantage:** Sponsoring Russian employers do not need to wait for annual quota lottery allocations. They can apply directly to the MVD for foreign hiring permits at any time during the calendar year, compressing recruitment lead times by 2 to 3 months.
- **Visa Structure:** Follows the same 90-day single entry to 1-year multiple entry extension architecture as the quota visa.

### Category 3: Highly Qualified Specialist (HQS / ВКС - Высококвалифицированный специалист)
Governed under **Article 13.2 of Federal Law No. 115-FZ**, the **HQS Visa** represents Russia's premier executive and senior technical immigration track:
- **Statutory Salary Threshold (2026 Standards):** Under amended federal migration legislation, the sponsoring employer must pay an HQS candidate a minimum quarterly gross salary of not less than **750,000 Russian Rubles** (equivalent to an average of **250,000 RUB / ~$2,700 USD / ~₹2,35,000 INR per month**).
- **Exclusive Legal Privileges:**
  - **3-Year Multi-Entry Work Visa:** Issued immediately for a 3-year term, eliminating the need for annual visa renewals.
  - **Zero Quota Constraints:** 100% exempt from federal or regional labor quota caps.
  - **Territorial Flexibility:** The HQS permit can authorize employment across multiple Russian federal subjects simultaneously if the employer operates regional branches.
  - **Family Sponsorship:** The HQS holder can sponsor their spouse, minor children, and dependent parents on 3-year accompanying dependent visas (*Chlen semi rabotnika*).
  - **Fast-Track Permanent Residence (*VNZh*):** HQS specialists and their families are legally entitled to apply directly for an indefinite **Russian Permanent Residence Permit (Вид на жительство - ВНЖ)** without first holding a Temporary Residence Permit (*RVP*).

### Stage 2: MVD Work Permit & Official Sovereign Work Invitation (*Priglashenie*)
This is the single most critical document in the entire Russian immigration process. **Without an authentic MVD Invitation, no Russian work visa can ever be issued.**
1. **Employer Application:** The Russian enterprise submits the candidate’s notarized passport translation, company quota certification, and corporate tax clearances to the **Main Directorate for Migration Affairs of the Ministry of Internal Affairs (ГУВМ МВД)** in the territory where the work will take place.
2. **The Deliverable (*Приглашение на въезд в РФ*):** The MVD issues the official work invitation in one of two formats:
   - **Yellow Security Paper (*Zhyolty Blank*):** A physical, high-security paper form printed on specialized government watermarked stock featuring an embossed official stamp, holographic migration seals, and a unique 9-digit sovereign serial number. The employer couriers the original physical yellow form to India.
   - **Electronic Invitation (*Elektronnoe Priglashenie*):** A digitally signed electronic document bearing a cryptographic MVD digital signature and an official verifiable 2D barcode.
3. **Purpose of Invitation:** The invitation explicitly states the purpose of entry as **"EMPLOYMENT" (НАЙМ / РАБОТА)**, designates the visa type as **Ordinary Work (Обыкновенная рабочая)**, and records the sponsoring Russian company name.

- **Validity Window:** The HIV medical certificate is valid for exactly **90 calendar days** from the date of the blood draw. It must state the candidate’s full passport details, date of birth, testing kit specifications, and bear the official seal and registration number of the diagnostic laboratory.
- **General Health Screening:** Candidates must also be certified free from active pulmonary tuberculosis (confirmed via chest radiography), syphilis, and leprosy.

### Stage 5: Indian Emigration Clearance (ECR vs. ECNR Protocols)
Under the **Indian Emigration Act of 1983**, administered by the **[Ministry of External Affairs (MEA)](https://emigrate.gov.in)** Overseas Employment Division:
- **ECNR Passport Holders:** Candidates who have completed 10th standard (Matriculation) or hold formal technical diplomas possess ECNR status and do not require formal PoE clearance. They can travel directly once their work visa and air tickets are confirmed.
