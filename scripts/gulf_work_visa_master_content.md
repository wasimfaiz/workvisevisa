## 1. GCC Statutory Framework: The 2026 Labor Landscape

The labor economies of the six GCC nations are undergoing the most ambitious structural overhaul in modern Middle Eastern history. Driven by long-term sovereign diversification initiatives—such as **Saudi Vision 2030**, **UAE Centennial 2071 & We the UAE 2031**, **Qatar National Vision 2030**, **Kuwait Vision 2035**, **Oman Vision 2040**, and **Bahrain Economic Vision 2030**—the region has instituted three transformative shifts:

### A. Nationalization Quotas vs. Foreign Workforce Integration
While all GCC countries maintain statutory citizen employment quotas—known as **Saudization (Nitaqat)** in KSA, **Emiratization (Nafis)** in the UAE, **Qatarization**, **Kuwaitization**, **Omanization**, and **Bahrainization**—these mandates primarily target clerical, retail, human resources, and entry-level executive roles. In contrast, the demand for foreign expertise in **civil engineering, MEP trades, specialized industrial construction, logistics, heavy automotive, green energy, oil and gas, and hospital care remains at an all-time high**. To hire an expatriate, companies must simply possess an approved commercial quota reflecting statutory compliance.

### B. Dismantling of the Traditional "Kafala" System
The archaic Kafala (sponsorship) system has been formally replaced across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and Bahrain by modern **statutory contractual models**:
- **Right to Change Employers:** Expatriate employees in Saudi Arabia can transfer jobs via **Qiwa** upon contract expiration or if the employer violates wage payment norms, without requiring an employer Non-Objection Certificate (NOC). Similar mobility rights exist in the UAE under **Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021** and in Qatar under **Law No. 19 of 2020**.
- **Exit & Re-entry Autonomy:** Foreign workers in Saudi Arabia and Qatar can independently apply for exit and re-entry permits or exit the country permanently via **Absher** or **Metrash2** without sovereign employer lockouts.
- **Strict Prohibition of Passport Confiscation:** Retaining an employee's original passport is a **criminal offense** across all GCC states, punishable by severe monetary fines (up to SAR 20,000 in KSA and AED 50,000 in the UAE) and commercial license blacklisting.

### C. Digital Integration & Real-Time Biometric Tracking
Every step of the overseas employment process—from the initial medical screening at **Wafid** centers in India to flight boarding, immigration electronic gates, and municipal resident cards (Iqama, Emirates ID, QID, Civil ID, Bataqa, CPR)—is now cryptographically linked. Paper visa stickers have been **100% abolished** in the UAE and Qatar, and are rapidly being phased out in Saudi Arabia and Oman in favor of biometric digital identities.

### A. Saudi Arabia (Kingdom of Saudi Arabia - KSA)

Saudi Arabia represents the largest employment market in the Middle East, driven by giga-projects like **[NEOM](/blogs/saudi-arabia-neom-work-visa-recruitment-guide-2026)**, the Red Sea Project, Qiddiya, ROSHN, Diriyah, and massive oil and gas turnaround operations under **Saudi Aramco**.

#### Statutory Process Flow:
1. **Commercial Visa Quota Approval:** The Saudi employer applies for an employment visa block on the **[Qiwa Platform](https://qiwa.sa)**. The establishment must maintain a "Platinum" or "Green" Nitaqat tier.
2. **Digital Qiwa Employment Contract:** The employer creates an official electronic contract specifying the exact trade, basic salary, allowances, duty hours (standard 8 hours/day, 48 hours/week), and probation period (maximum 90 days, extendable to 180 days by mutual written agreement). The worker reviews and electronically authenticates this contract.
3. **Takamol SVP Trade Test:** For 23+ designated artisanal trades, the worker must pass a computerized theory test and hands-on practical assessment at an accredited Indian test center. The resulting digital certificate automatically syncs with Qiwa.
4. **Wafid Medical Fitness Certificate:** The candidate completes the mandatory medical screening at an authorized GAMCA center in India, receiving an electronic "FIT" status on **[wafid.com](https://wafid.com)**.
5. **Electronic Visa Authorization (Wakalat / MOFA):** The employer issues an electronic power of attorney (Wakalat) via **[visa.mofa.gov.sa](https://visa.mofa.gov.sa)** to an authorized recruiting agency like WorkWise Visa.
6. **Consular Visa Stamping (VFS TasHeel):** The candidate's original passport, Wafid medical slip, Takamol certificate, and police clearance certificate (if required for the specific profession) are lodged for consular visa stamping.
7. **Port Entry & Muqeem Registration:** Within 72 hours prior to arrival at King Khalid International Airport (Riyadh), King Abdulaziz International Airport (Jeddah), or King Fahd International Airport (Dammam), the candidate is registered on the **[Muqeem Portal](https://muqeem.sa)**.
8. **Iqama Issuance:** Under Article 40 of the Saudi Labor Law, the employer must complete the post-arrival medical check, activate Council of Cooperative Health Insurance (CCHI) coverage, and issue the physical/digital **Iqama (Muqeem Resident Card)** within **90 days of arrival**. Failure to do so incurs an automatic penalty of SAR 500 on the employer.

### B. United Arab Emirates (UAE)

The UAE—led by Dubai and Abu Dhabi—operates the most automated and expatriate-friendly labor ecosystem in the world, governed by **Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021** and **Cabinet Resolution No. 65 of 2022**.

#### Statutory Process Flow:
1. **MOHRE Preliminary Quota & Job Offer:** The mainland company applies to MOHRE for an electronic quota and issues a standardized bilingual **MOHRE Job Offer Letter**. Both parties sign this document before any visa processing begins.
2. **Employment Entry Permit (60 Days):** MOHRE issues an initial electronic work permit, and the **[GDRFA Dubai](https://www.gdrfad.gov.ae)** or **[ICP UAE](https://icp.gov.ae)** generates a **60-Day Electronic Employment Entry Permit** (formerly known as the "Pink Visa").
3. **Port Arrival or In-Country Status Change:**
   - *From Abroad:* The candidate boards their flight to DXB/AUH with the digital entry permit PDF and enters through immigration e-gates.
   - *Inside UAE:* If already in Dubai on a tourist or visit visa, an **In-Country Status Modification (Tajdeed Al Wadha)** is filed via Amer without leaving the country (government fee: AED 650–750).
4. **DHA / SEHA Medical Fitness Screening:** The candidate undergoes venous blood testing (HIV, Syphilis, Hepatitis B/C for food/health/domestic trades) and digital chest radiography for pulmonary TB at an authorized center (e.g., Al Muhaisnah, Smart Salem, or SEHA clinics).
5. **Emirates ID Biometric Enrollment:** The candidate visits an ICP Customer Happiness Center for 10-digit fingerprint scanning, iris scanning, and facial biometrics.
6. **Mandatory Health Insurance Activation:** Compliant health insurance (Essential Benefits Plan - EBP or comprehensive corporate cover) is bound and synced via the DHA Basmah system.
7. **Digital Residence Permit Issuance:** Physical passport visa stickers have been **100% abolished**. The digital residency is generated in the GDRFA/ICP systems, and the physical smart **Emirates ID card** is delivered by Emirates Post within 3–5 working days.

### C. State of Qatar

Qatar's employment infrastructure is famous for its groundbreaking pre-departure processing model operated through dedicated **Qatar Visa Centers (QVC)** located directly across major Indian metropolitan cities.

#### Statutory Process Flow:
1. **Ministry of Labour (MoL) Approval:** The Qatari sponsor secures an approved labor block quota through the **[Ministry of Labour (MoL Qatar)](https://www.mol.gov.qa)** and initiates the work visa on the **[Ministry of Interior (MoI Qatar)](https://portal.moi.gov.qa)** portal.
2. **QVC Pre-Departure Appointment in India:** The candidate visits an official **[Qatar Visa Center (QVC)](https://www.qatarvisacenter.com)** in Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kochi, or Lucknow. At the QVC, three mandatory steps occur in a single visit:
   - **Biometric Enrollment:** 10 digital fingerprints, facial photography, and iris scanning.
   - **Comprehensive Medical Examination:** Venous blood draw and chest X-ray performed strictly to Hamad Medical Corporation (HMC) standards.
   - **Electronic Employment Contract Signing:** The worker reviews their bilingual employment contract in Hindi, Urdu, Tamil, or English, verifies wage figures and job roles, and applies a digital biometric signature.
3. **MoI Work Visa Issuance:** Once QVC clearance is stamped, the MoI electronically issues the formal **Qatar Employment Entry Visa**.
4. **Port Entry via Hamad International Airport (DOH):** The candidate travels to Doha. 
5. **Qatar ID (QID) Issuance:** Within 30 days of arrival, the employer activates the worker's government health card (Hamad Health Card) and delivers the physical **Qatar ID (QID)** smart card.
6. **Kafala Freedom:** Under Qatari Law No. 19 of 2020, workers do not require an Exit Permit to depart Qatar and are legally permitted to change employers after completing their probation period by submitting an electronic notification via the MoL portal.

### D. State of Kuwait

Kuwait maintains one of the highest currency valuation regimes in the world (1 KWD ≈ ₹275+ INR). In 2026, the Kuwaiti Government—under the directives of the **Public Authority for Manpower (PAM)** and the **Ministry of Interior (MoI)**—has reopened foreign work permit quotas with heightened educational scrutiny and mandatory police clearance.

#### Statutory Process Flow:
1. **PAM Initial Work Permit Approval:** The Kuwaiti company files for an electronic work permit through PAM's **Ashal Portal** under an active commercial or government contract quota.
2. **Mandatory Educational & Professional Attestation:** For technical, supervisory, and engineering roles, degrees must complete the full 5-stage legalization chain (Notary &rarr; State HRD &rarr; MEA &rarr; Kuwait Embassy New Delhi/Mumbai). Engineers must obtain formal accreditation from the **[Kuwait Society of Engineers (KSE)](https://kse.org.kw)** before PAM approves the permit.
3. **Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) from PSK:** Every Indian applicant for a Kuwait work visa must present a valid PCC issued by the **[Passport Seva Kendra (PSK)](https://www.passportindia.gov.in)**, verified by the MEA and stamped by the Kuwait Embassy.
4. **Wafid Medical Examination:** The applicant undergoes medical testing at an authorized GAMCA center in India, clearing blood and chest radiologic tests.
5. **MoI Work Visa (Article 18 Visa):** PAM transmits approval to the Ministry of Interior, which issues the electronic **Article 18 Employment Visa**.
6. **Consular Visa Stamping & Port Entry:** The visa is stamped through the Kuwait Embassy or VFS Kuwait. The candidate travels to Kuwait International Airport (KWI).
7. **Civil ID via PACI & Sahel Kuwait App:** Within 60 days, the candidate completes local fingerprint registration at the General Department of Criminal Evidence, activates health insurance, and receives their physical **Kuwait Civil ID Card** from the **[Public Authority for Civil Information (PACI)](https://www.paci.gov.kw)** while registering on the sovereign **Sahel Kuwait App**.

### E. Sultanate of Oman

Oman offers an exceptionally stable and respected employment ecosystem, regulated by the **Ministry of Labour (MoL Oman)** and the **Royal Oman Police (ROP)** under the modernized **Oman Labour Law (Royal Decree No. 52/2023)**.

#### Statutory Process Flow:
1. **Ministry of Labour Clearance:** The Omani enterprise applies for a labor clearance license (*Tasreeh*) via the **[MoL Portal](https://www.mol.gov.om)**, verifying that the company complies with statutory Omanization percentages for the designated trade.
2. **Wafid Medical Fitness Clearance:** The Indian applicant takes the Wafid GAMCA medical test in India, ensuring zero communicable disease indicators.
3. **ROP Employment Visa Application:** With MoL clearance and Wafid medical fit status uploaded, the employer submits the visa application to the **[Royal Oman Police (ROP)](https://www.rop.gov.om)** via an authorized **Sanad Service Center**.
4. **Consular Stamping & Travel:** The ROP issues the electronic **Employment Visa**. The worker completes biometric visa processing and travels to Muscat International Airport (MCT) or Salalah Airport (SLL).
5. **Resident Card (Bataqa) Issuance:** Within 30 days of arrival, the worker visits the ROP Civil Status Directorate for biometric fingerprinting, iris capture, and issuance of the physical smart **Oman Resident Card (Bataqa)**.
6. **Statutory Protections:** Under Royal Decree 52/2023, employees enjoy 30 days of paid annual leave, mandatory paid sick leave up to 10 weeks, and strict Wage Protection System (WPS) bank salary disbursement.

### F. Kingdom of Bahrain

Bahrain operates an agile, business-friendly immigration system managed by the **Labour Market Regulatory Authority (LMRA)** in coordination with the **Nationality, Passports and Residence Affairs (NPRA)**.

#### Statutory Process Flow:
1. **LMRA Work Permit Filing:** The Bahraini employer logs into the **[LMRA Expat Management System (EMS)](https://lmra.gov.bh)** and submits an electronic work permit application, attaching the candidate's passport copy, attested educational qualifications (if professional), and formal employment contract.
2. **Integrated Security & NPRA Clearance:** LMRA coordinates background clearance with NPRA electronically, eliminating dual ministerial filings.
3. **Wafid Pre-Departure Medical in India:** The candidate completes the Wafid GAMCA medical examination in India. The fit certificate is automatically synchronized into the LMRA database.
4. **LMRA Electronic Work Visa Generation:** LMRA approves the work permit and issues the digital visa authorization document.
5. **Arrival via Bahrain International Airport (BAH):** The worker enters Bahrain. At airport immigration, biometric enrollment is finalized at the dedicated LMRA counter.
6. **Post-Arrival Medical & CPR Card Issuance:** The worker completes a local confirmatory health check at an authorized Bahrain health center. ## 3. Wafid (Formerly GAMCA) Medical Examination: Rules, Tests & Blacklist Risks

The **Wafid** system—formerly known as GAMCA (Gulf Approved Medical Centres Association) and operated under the sovereign auspices of the **[GCC Health Council](https://wafid.com)**—is the centralized, non-negotiable medical gatekeeper for all foreign workers entering Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, and Bahrain.

> 🏥 **Wafid (GAMCA) Pre-Departure Medical Workflow:**  
> **1. Online Registration** *($10 USD fee at wafid.com)* &rarr; **2. Random Clinic Allocation** *(in Delhi, Mumbai, Patna, Lucknow, etc.)* &rarr; **3. Clinical, Blood & Chest X-Ray Tests** &rarr; **4. Electronic Evaluation** &rarr; **5. GCC Portal Clearance OR 2-Year Medical Ban**

| Wafid Diagnostic Stage | Medical Scope & Parameters Screened | Normal Clearance ("FIT") | Disqualifying Outcome ("UNFIT") |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Physical Examination** | Blood pressure, heart rate, hernia, surgical scars, vision | Normal vitals, zero active abdominal hernia | Severe decompensated cardiovascular failure |
| **Serology & Blood Work** | HIV 1/2, Hepatitis B (HBsAg), Hepatitis C, Syphilis (VDRL) | All viral serology markers completely negative | Confirmed HIV, active Hepatitis B/C, Syphilis |
| **Digital Chest X-Ray** | Lungs evaluated for active tuberculosis (TB) or calcifications | Clear lung fields, no active lesions | Active pulmonary TB or unappealed dense scarring |
| **Biochemical Panels** | Fasting blood glucose, liver enzymes (ALT/AST), creatinine | Blood sugar and renal profiles within normal range | Severe uncontrolled diabetes or renal collapse |
| **Final Registry Sync** | Electronic transmission to unified GCC Health Council registry | **Instant "FIT" Certificate** unlocked for visa stamping | **Automatic 2-Year GCC Immigration Blacklist** |

### A. How Wafid Works: The Blind Computer Allocation System
- Applicants cannot choose their preferred clinic. You must log onto **[wafid.com](https://wafid.com)**, input your passport details, nationality, and city of choice (e.g., Patna, Delhi, Mumbai, Lucknow, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kochi, Bangalore, Ahmedabad), and pay the standard registration fee of **$10 USD** via international debit/credit card.
- The computer algorithm randomly assigns you to an accredited diagnostic laboratory in that city.
- You must report to the assigned clinic with your original passport, 4 passport-size white-background photographs, and the printed Wafid appointment slip.

### B. Exhaustive Battery of Medical Tests
Every candidate undergoes three diagnostic tiers:
1. **Physical Examination:** Blood pressure, resting heart rate, visual acuity (color blindness check for electricians and drivers), physical hernia check, surgical scar inspection, and varicose veins examination.
2. **Serological & Blood Testing:**
   - **HIV 1 & 2 Antibodies (ELISA / Western Blot):** Absolute exclusion if positive.
   - **Hepatitis B Surface Antigen (HBsAg):** Absolute exclusion if positive across all GCC states.
   - **Hepatitis C Antibodies (Anti-HCV):** Absolute exclusion across all GCC states.
   - **Syphilis (VDRL / TPHA):** Non-negotiable exclusion unless proven treated with historic negative titers (varies by country).
   - **Fasting Blood Glucose (HbA1c):** Evaluated for severe uncontrolled diabetes mellitus.
   - **Renal & Liver Function Panels:** Creatinine, urea, bilirubin, SGOT/SGPT enzymes.
   - **Microfilaria & Malaria Smear:** Screened via peripheral blood smear.
3. **Digital Chest Radiography (X-Ray):**
   - Screened for active pulmonary tuberculosis (TB), lung cavitation, pleural effusion, and **healed or inactive calcified tubercular scars**.

### C. The Fit vs. Unfit Criteria & TB Scar Realities
- **Active Infections:** Any confirmed diagnosis of HIV, active pulmonary tuberculosis, Hepatitis B, or Hepatitis C results in an immediate **"UNFIT"** declaration.
- **The Healed TB Scar Dilemma:** Historically, any visible calcification or fibrous scar on a chest X-ray resulted in an immediate permanent disqualification. In 2026:
  - For the **UAE**, old inactive scars may be evaluated via GeneXpert sputum PCR at DHA clinics upon arrival, leading to conditional surveillance permits.
  - For **Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, and Qatar**, a scarred chest X-ray at a Wafid center in India will almost always trigger an **"UNFIT"** status unless a clear sputum test and specialist pulmonologist certificate are accepted by the medical board.
- **Consequences of an "UNFIT" Stamp:**
  - An "UNFIT" medical result is electronically uploaded to the unified GCC Health Council database within 24 hours.
  - The candidate's passport number is automatically placed on a **2-Year GCC-wide Medical Blacklist**, preventing visa issuance across all six nations during this period.
  - **Never use forged medical slips:** Border authorities verify Wafid QR codes live at immigration. Traveling with a forged medical slip results in immediate arrest, deportation, and a lifetime criminal ban.

If an infection is detected, you can treat it prior to official GCC logging. - ❌ **"Tourist Visa to Employment Visa" Promises with No Employer Contract:** Traveling on a 30-day visit visa without an authenticated MOHRE offer letter or Qiwa contract leaves you vulnerable to exploitation, illegal labor status, and arrest.

### How to Verify Genuine Visas Directly on Government Portals:

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions (25 Technical FAQs)

### Q1: Can I convert a tourist visa or visit visa into an employment visa in Dubai without leaving the UAE?
**Answer:** **Yes.** Under UAE immigration regulations managed by GDRFA and Amer, you do not need to exit the country. Once your employer receives your 60-Day Employment Entry Permit, an **In-Country Status Change (Tagheer Al Wadha)** application is submitted electronically (government fee: AED 650–750). Your status transitions immediately to pre-residency, allowing you to proceed with your DHA medical screening and Emirates ID biometrics.

### Q2: What happens if I am declared medically "UNFIT" at a Wafid GAMCA center in India?
**Answer:** An "UNFIT" result is uploaded to the unified GCC Health Council database (**wafid.com**) within 24 hours. Your passport number is placed on an automatic **2-Year GCC Immigration Blacklist**, blocking work visa processing across all six Gulf nations. If you suspect a false positive (e.g., an inactive lung calcification or borderline blood sugar), you can request an appeal review with the GCC Health Council medical board or re-test after the 2-year cooling-off window.

### Q3: Is the Takamol Skill Verification Program (SVP) mandatory for all Gulf countries?
**Answer:** Currently, Takamol SVP is strictly mandatory for **Saudi Arabia (KSA)** across 23+ designated technical trades (electricians, welders, plumbers, HVAC technicians, auto mechanics, carpenters). Other GCC nations—including the UAE and Kuwait—are piloting similar competency test models with India's National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC), but have not yet made it universally compulsory for all private sector entries.

### Q4: Can an employer in Saudi Arabia or the UAE legally keep my passport?
**Answer:** **No, absolutely not.** Retaining an employee's passport is a **criminal violation** under Article 40 of Saudi Labor Law and UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021. Employers face monetary fines ranging from SAR 5,000 to SAR 20,000 in KSA and up to AED 50,000 in the UAE per confiscated passport. If your sponsor refuses to return your passport, you can file a confidential complaint via the **MOHRE Smart App** or the **Qiwa Platform**.

### Q5: What is the difference between ECR and ECNR passport holders under the Indian Emigration Act?
**Answer:** ECR (Emigration Check Required) passports are issued to citizens who have not passed Class 10 (Matriculation). They must secure mandatory **PoE Emigration Clearance** through the eMigrate portal and purchase a **Pravasi Bharatiya Bima Yojana (PBBY)** insurance policy before flying for employment to any of the 18 designated ECR nations (including all 6 GCC states). ECNR (Non-ECR) passport holders have completed Class 10 or higher and can travel directly without PoE clearance.

### Q6: Can I change my job in Saudi Arabia without my current employer's consent (NOC)?
**Answer:** **Yes, under specific conditions.** Under Saudi Arabia's Labor Reform Initiative enacted on the **Qiwa platform**, an employee can transfer their Iqama to a new employer without current sponsor approval if: (1) Their digital contract has expired, (2) The employer fails to pay their monthly salary for three consecutive months through WPS, (3) The employer fails to renew their Iqama within 90 days of expiration, or (4) The employer fails to attend two labor dispute settlement sessions.

### Q7: What is the Qatar Visa Center (QVC) process, and who pays the appointment fees?
**Answer:** QVC is Qatar's integrated pre-departure processing center located in Indian cities. The candidate undergoes biometric capture, medical examination, and signs their electronic employment contract before departing India. **The Qatari employer is legally mandated to pay the entire QVC processing fee (QAR 495)** directly through the MoI portal. Candidates do not pay any fee at the QVC center.

### Q8: What are the degree attestation requirements for engineers moving to Kuwait?
**Answer:** Engineers moving to Kuwait face stringent accreditation standards. The original B.Tech / B.E. degree must be authenticated by the State HRD, MEA New Delhi, and the Kuwait Embassy. Additionally, the engineer must be formally accredited by the **Kuwait Society of Engineers (KSE)**, which verifies that the awarding Indian university and program are listed on the official AICTE / NBA accredited list. Without KSE clearance, PAM will not issue an engineer work permit.

### Q9: What is the Wage Protection System (WPS) and how does it protect expatriate salaries?
**Answer:** The WPS is an electronic salary transfer monitoring system operated jointly by the Ministries of Labor and Central Banks in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and Oman. Employers must disburse 100% of agreed monthly salaries through registered financial institutions or payroll cards. If an employer defaults or delays payment, the central government algorithm automatically freezes the company's file, blocking new visa issuance and allowing unpaid workers to legally transfer to new jobs.

### Q10: How much is the overstay fine if my Gulf work visa or visit visa expires?
**Answer:** 
- In the **UAE**, overstay fines are standardized at **AED 50 per day** following the expiration of the statutory grace period (30 days for tourists, 60–180 days for cancelled residents).
- In **Saudi Arabia**, overstaying an exit-re-entry visa or Iqama incurs fines starting at **SAR 1,000**, escalating to deportation and a 3-year entry ban.
- In **Qatar**, overstay fines are **QAR 200 per day** for unauthorized residence.

### Q11: What is the minimum basic salary required to sponsor a wife and children in Dubai?
**Answer:** A male resident holding a valid UAE residence permit must earn a minimum basic monthly salary of **AED 4,000**, or **AED 3,000 plus company-provided furnished accommodation**, to sponsor his wife and children. Sponsoring dependent parents requires a minimum salary of **AED 20,000** and a registered two-bedroom residential tenancy contract (Ejari).

### Q12: Can I legally drive a vehicle in Saudi Arabia or UAE with an Indian driving license?
**Answer:** You cannot drive a private commercial vehicle on an Indian license once you hold resident status. However, expatriates holding an Indian driving license can utilize the **"Golden Chance" Scheme** in Dubai (a one-time direct theory and road test through RTA without mandatory driving school classes) to secure a UAE Driving License. In Saudi Arabia, candidates must complete light or heavy driving school training through Dallah Driving Academy before an official Saudi license is issued.

### Q13: What happens during the midday work ban in the Gulf during summer?
**Answer:** Between **June 15 and September 15**, all outdoor physical labor in direct sunlight is strictly prohibited across all GCC states between **12:00 PM and 3:00 PM (or 3:30 PM)**. Companies must adjust shift schedules to early morning or evening hours and provide shaded rest areas. Violating companies face heavy financial penalties (up to AED 50,000 in UAE and SAR 10,000 per worker in KSA) and site suspension.

### Q14: How is End-of-Service Gratuity calculated in the UAE under the new labor law?
**Answer:** Under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021, if an employee completes at least one full year of continuous service, they are entitled to end-of-service severance pay calculated on their last drawn basic salary:
- **21 Calendar Days of Basic Salary** for each year of the first 5 years of service.
- **30 Calendar Days of Basic Salary** for each additional year beyond 5 years.
The total gratuity amount cannot exceed two full years' worth of basic salary.

### Q15: Is a Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) required for all Gulf work visas?
**Answer:** A PCC from the **Passport Seva Kendra (PSK)** is strictly mandatory for **Kuwait work visas (Article 18)**. It is also required for select security, banking, healthcare, and educational categories in the **UAE** and **Saudi Arabia**, and for Indian candidates seeking eMigrate clearance under specific occupational codes. Consult our [PCC Master Guide](/blogs/police-clearance-certificate-pcc-gulf-work-visas-passport-office-guide-2026) for complete booking protocols.

### Q16: Can I apply for a Gulf work visa if I have a tattoo or healed surgical scars?
**Answer:** Tattoos generally do not disqualify blue-collar or technical candidates during the Wafid medical exam unless they are infected or located in sensitive professional areas (e.g., flight crew, luxury hospitality). Surgical scars are evaluated during the physical exam to ensure full abdominal wall healing without active hernia risks. If you had prior lung surgery, obtain a certified hospital discharge summary before taking your Wafid chest X-ray.

### Q17: What is the validity period of an Iqama in Saudi Arabia?
**Answer:** Standard private sector employment Iqamas are issued with an annual validity of **1 Year (12 Months)**, renewed annually by the employer upon payment of the municipal fee, CCHI medical insurance, and MHRSD expatriate levy (*Maktab Amal* fee). In recent years, employers can also renew Iqamas in flexible increments of 3, 6, 9, or 12 months.

### Q18: Can a company deduct recruitment fees or visa stamping charges from my salary?
**Answer:** **No, it is strictly illegal.** Under Article 6 of UAE Labour Law, Article 40 of Saudi Labour Law, and Qatari immigration statutes, the employer is legally obligated to fund 100% of all visa quota charges, entry permits, resident ID card fees, and standard annual medical insurance. Any contract clause stipulating salary deductions for visa costs is legally void.

### Q19: What is the Pravasi Bharatiya Bima Yojana (PBBY) and how do I claim benefits?
**Answer:** The PBBY is a mandatory government-backed insurance policy for ECR passport holders deployed through the eMigrate portal. Costing roughly ₹275 to ₹375 for 2 to 3 years, it provides ₹10 Lakh coverage for accidental death or permanent disability abroad, ₹1 Lakh emergency hospitalization cover, and guaranteed repatriation costs. Claims can be filed through the Indian Embassy's **Madad Portal (madad.gov.in)** or the designated Indian insurance provider.

### Q20: How can I verify if a job demand from a recruiting agency in India is genuine?
**Answer:** Verify that the recruiting agency possesses an active **Registration Certificate (RC) number** issued by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India. You can check the agent's validity and approved quota demands on **[emigrate.gov.in](https://emigrate.gov.in)** under "Search Registered Agencies". WorkWise Visa operates as a fully compliant, authorized overseas recruitment entity.

### Q21: What is the probation period in Saudi Arabia and the UAE?
**Answer:** In both Saudi Arabia and the UAE, the statutory probation period is a maximum of **6 months (180 days)**. During probation, the employer evaluates employee performance. In the UAE, if an employer terminates a worker during probation, they must provide at least 14 days' written notice. If a worker resigns during probation to join another UAE employer, the new employer must compensate the former employer for recruitment expenses.

### Q22: Are domestic workers (housemaids, nannies, private drivers) covered by the standard labor law?
**Answer:** Domestic workers are governed by dedicated sovereign statutes—such as **UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 9 of 2022 on Domestic Workers** and Saudi Domestic Worker Regulations. Recruitment must occur exclusively through accredited government-licensed centers (e.g., **Tadbeer Centers** in the UAE and **Musaned** in Saudi Arabia), which mandate 12 hours of daily rest, paid weekly off, private quarters, and timely wage disbursement.

### Q23: What should I do if my employer in the Gulf defaults on paying my monthly salary?
**Answer:** You should immediately lodge a formal grievance through the sovereign labor mobile app:
- *Saudi Arabia:* File via the **Qiwa Portal** or call MHRSD helpline **19911**.
- *UAE:* File a labor dispute via the **MOHRE App** or call **800 60**.
- *Qatar:* Submit a complaint through the **MoL Grievance Portal** or the **Metrash2 App**.
Labor departments inspect WPS bank records and mandate employer settlement within 7 to 14 days. If unresolved, the case is referred to the specialized Labor Court, and you are granted immediate authorization to transfer to a new sponsor.

### Q24: What is the GCC Unified Tourist Visa ("Grand Tours") and can I use it to work?
**Answer:** The GCC Unified Tourist Visa (modeled after the European Schengen visa) allows international tourists to travel freely across all six GCC nations on a single visa. **However, it strictly prohibits commercial employment.** Engaging in paid work on any tourist or visit visa is a severe immigration offense, leading to immediate deportation, fines, and lifetime GCC-wide labor bans.
