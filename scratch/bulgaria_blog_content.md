## Legal Framework: The Bulgarian Single Residence and Work Permit (ERPP)

Under Article 24i of the **Foreigners in the Republic of Bulgaria Act (*ZChRB*)**, third-country nationals (including Indian citizens) seeking long-term employment must obtain a **Single Residence and Work Permit (*Единно разрешение за пребиваване и работа - ERPP*)**.

### Key Statutory Provisions of the ERPP:
1. **Centralized One-Stop Procedure:** The permit application is initiated directly in Bulgaria by the employer at the regional department of the **Migration Directorate (*Дирекция „Миграция“*)** within the Ministry of Interior (**MVR**).
2. **Dual-Agency Assessment:** The Migration Directorate coordinates directly with the **Employment Agency (*Агенция по заетостта - AZ*)**, which reviews the company's financial health, genuine labor requirement, and national labor market conditions.
3. **Statutory Validity:** The ERPP is issued for a duration of **up to 3 years** (or the duration of the employment contract), renewable as long as employment continues.
4. **Mandatory National Type D Visa:** Once the Migration Directorate approves the ERPP file, it transmits an official positive decision (*Решение*). The Indian candidate then submits their passport at **VFS Global India** to obtain the **National Long-Stay Type D Visa (Visa D)**, authorizing border entry into Bulgaria.
5. **Final Biometric Card (TRC):** Within 14 days of landing in Bulgaria, the employee visits the local Migration department to complete biometrics and receive their plastic **Biometric Residence Card (*Карта за продължително пребиваване*)**.

## Critical Anti-Scam Advisory: 6 Red Flags of Fake Bulgarian Work Permits

Because Bulgaria is now part of the Schengen Area, unscrupulous agents are exploiting unaware jobseekers across rural Bihar, Eastern UP, Punjab, and Rajasthan. Protect yourself by recognizing these 6 unmistakable signs of visa fraud:

### 1. The "Tourist Visa to Work Permit Conversion" Scam
**The Trap:** An agent tells you, *"Bhaiya, Bulgaria ka tourist visa le lo, wahan pahunch kar company mein work permit lagwa denge."*
**The Ground Reality:** **100% ILLEGAL.** Under Article 24 of the Foreigners in the Republic of Bulgaria Act, third-country nationals entering Bulgaria on a short-stay Type C tourist visa or visit visa are strictly prohibited from converting their status to an ERPP work permit inside the country. You will be denied, declared an illegal migrant, fined, and deported with an 3-to-5-year Schengen entry ban.

### 2. Fake Migration Directorate Letterheads with Broken Cyrillic or Bad English
**The Trap:** Agents share a colorful PDF with Bulgarian flags, gold foil eagle stamps, and English text claiming to be a "Bulgarian Work Permit Certificate."
**The Ground Reality:** Official Bulgarian government documents are issued in the **Bulgarian language (Cyrillic script)** under the official title:
> **„РЕШЕНИЕ ЗА ПРЕДОСТАВЯНЕ НА ЕДИННО РАЗРЕШЕНИЕ ЗА ПРЕБИВАВАНЕ И РАБОТА“**

Official decisions bear a verifiable administrative reference number (*Регистрационен номер*), the unique identification code of the employer (*ЕИК / БУЛСТАТ*), the official round wet seal of the Ministry of Interior (*Министерство на вътрешните работи*), and the digital signature or stamp of the Director of the Migration Directorate.

### 3. Fictitious Employer Names Not Found on the Commercial Register
Before making any financial commitment, ask your agent for the Bulgarian employer's 9-digit **Unified Identification Code (*Единен идентификационен код - ЕИК / UIC*)**.
Go to the **Bulgarian Commercial Register portal ([portal.registryagency.bg/CR](https://portal.registryagency.bg/CR))** and enter the EIK number. If the company is listed as inactive, suspended, in liquidation, or cannot be found, the job offer is 100% fraudulent.

### 4. Demanding Massive Advance Cash Fees Before Official Approval
Legitimate European recruitment agencies licensed by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) do not demand hundreds of thousands of rupees before your file is officially registered. Sponsoring Bulgarian employers cover the state application filing fees in Bulgaria.

### 5. Fake Job Offers with "No Experience, No Trade Test, High Salary"
If an agent offers an unskilled packaging or general helper job paying **€3,500 EUR (₹3.2 Lakh/month)** in Bulgaria with "free flight tickets, free food, and no interview," you are dealing with a scam. As documented in our salary benchmarks, standard Bulgarian industrial wages range from **1,300 to 3,200 BGN (₹48,000 to ₹1,20,000 INR)**. Anyone promising Western European IT executive salaries for basic factory work is attempting to steal your money.

After securing a Bulgarian TRC card, you can submit your apostilled Indian driving license and medical fitness certificate to the Traffic Police (*Пътна полиция - КАТ*) for formal exchange into an EU driving license.

Your contributions are logged in the National Revenue Agency (**NRA / НАП**) under your personal foreigner registration number (**ЛНЧ - Личен номер на чужденец**). These records are permanently registered on the EU social security network, protecting your pension entitlements.

### Q11: What is the difference between a Type D Visa and a Biometric TRC Card?
- **Type D Visa (Виза тип D):** A multi-entry sticker affixed into your Indian passport by the Bulgarian Embassy in New Delhi, valid for 180 days. Its sole legal purpose is to grant you authorized border entry into Bulgaria.
- **Biometric TRC Card (Карта за продължително пребиваване):** A credit-card-sized biometric identity card issued by the Migration Directorate in Bulgaria after you land. It serves as your official legal resident ID card inside Europe, valid for up to 3 years.

### Q12: Do I need an MEA Apostille on my Indian Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)?
**Yes, strictly mandatory.** Because Bulgaria is a contracting state to the Hague Apostille Convention of 5 October 1961, the Bulgarian Migration Directorate and Embassy require that your PSK Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) carry the physical square **Hague Apostille sticker issued by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India**. State-level notary stamps or police stamps without MEA Apostille will be immediately rejected.
