The 7-Stage UAE 2-Year Employment Visa Roadmap\n\nSecuring a genuine 2-Year Residence & Work Visa in the UAE follows a rigid, sequentially locked administrative pathway. No candidate should ever board a flight to Dubai or Abu Dhabi on a tourist or visit visa expecting a legitimate corporate onboarding.\n\n```\n[Stage 1: Electronic Job Offer Letter (MOHRE Quota & E-Signature)]\n                          ↓\n[Stage 2: Employment Entry Permit Issuance (Pink Visa / eVisa via GDRFA or ICP)]\n                          ↓\n[Stage 3: Arrival in UAE & In-Country Change of Status]\n                          ↓\n[Stage 4: Medical Fitness Examination (Blood Panel & Chest X-Ray at DHA/EHS)]\n                          ↓\n[Stage 5: Emirates ID Biometrics & Digital Registration (ICP Center)]\n                          ↓\n[Stage 6: Tawjeeh Labor Orientation & ILOE Insurance Enrollment]\n                          ↓\n[Stage 7: Electronic Labor Card Issuance & Residency Stamping on ICP App]\n```\n\n---\n\n### Stage 1: MOHRE Electronic Job Offer Letter (MB Form)\nBefore applying for your visa, your employing company must have an active, government-approved expatriate labor quota. The employer generates an official **Standard Employment Offer Letter (MB Form)** through the MOHRE portal.\n- **Bi-Lingual Mandate:** The offer letter is printed in both English and the candidate’s mother tongue (Hindi or Urdu).\n- **Binding Contractual Terms:** It explicitly defines the basic salary, housing allowance, transport allowance, food subsidy, weekly rest day (Friday/Sunday), and working hours (standard 8 hours/day).\n- **Candidate E-Signature:** The candidate must physically sign or digitally fingerprint this exact document. It is illegal for any employer or recruiting agency to alter the terms, salary, or trade category once the signed offer letter is submitted to MOHRE.\n- **Verification:** You can verify your offer letter live on `mohre.gov.ae` using your Transaction Number (ST Number) and Passport Number.\n\n---\n\n### Stage 2: Employment Entry Permit Issuance (Pink Visa)\nOnce the signed MOHRE offer is validated by the labor ministry, the employer submits an application to immigration:\n- For **Dubai** companies: Applied through **GDRFA Dubai**.\n- For **Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, and Umm Al Quwain**: Applied through **ICP Smart Services**.\n- **Document Output:** An electronic **Employment Entry Permit** (historically referred to as the \"Pink Visa\"). This document has a validity of **60 days** from the date of issuance, during which the worker must enter the UAE to complete residency formalities.\n- **ECR Emigration Clearance (For India):** Candidates holding an ECR (Non-Matric) passport must have their employment permit registered on the Government of India’s **[eMigrate](https://emigrate.gov.in)** portal by a licensed recruitment agency to secure mandatory Emigration Clearance (POE) and ₹10 Lakh Pravasi Bharatiya Bima Yojana (PBBY) insurance before airport departure.\n\n---\n\n### Stage 3: UAE Arrival & Change of Status\nUpon landing at Dubai International (DXB), Abu Dhabi Zayed International (AUH), or Sharjah International (SHJ), the immigration officer scans your entry permit barcode.\n- If the candidate was already inside the UAE (e.g. transitioning from a completed previous work contract): An official **Change of Status (Ta'deel Al Wad')** fee is paid to convert their legal stay to the new employment visa without exiting the country.\n- The candidate now has **60 calendar days** to complete all medical, biometrics, and labor card requirements.\n\n---\n\n### Stage 4: Pre-Employment Medical Fitness Examination\nThe UAE maintains rigorous public health screening protocols. Candidates must visit an authorized government medical fitness center (e.g., Al Muhaisnah or Salem Smart Centers in Dubai; Capital Health Screening or SEHA in Abu Dhabi):\n1. **Venipuncture Blood Test:** Screens for Infectious Diseases including HIV (1 & 2) and Hepatitis B surface antigen (mandatory for food handlers, salon workers, nursery staff, and healthcare personnel).\n2. **Chest X-Ray:** Screens for active Pulmonary Tuberculosis (TB). \n   - *2026 Medical Protocol Update:* Candidates displaying inactive, healed fibrous scars from old, cured childhood lung infections are subjected to automated Sputum Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) analysis. If verified non-infectious, they are granted fitness certificates under conditional medical supervision rather than immediate deportation.\n   - Any worker diagnosed with active, communicable TB or HIV is declared \"Unfit\" and repatriated under sovereign protocols.\n\n---\n\n### Stage 5: Emirates ID Biometrics at ICP Service Center\nYour **Emirates Identity Card (EID)** is the single most important sovereign identification document in the UAE. It is required to open bank accounts, receive salary transfers, sign rental leases, and access government clinics.\n- The employer types the official Emirates ID application form through the ICP portal.\n- The worker visits an authorized ICP Customer Happiness Center for biometric capture: **10-digit digital fingerprint scanning**, digital signature capture, and high-resolution facial iris photography.\n\n---\n\n### Stage 6: Tawjeeh Labor Orientation & ILOE Insurance\nUnder Ministry of Human Resources regulations:\n- **Tawjeeh Training:** Non-exempt trade workers and technicians must attend a mandatory 90-minute legal orientation session at an accredited **Tawjeeh Center**. The curriculum—conducted in Hindi, Bengali, Malayalam, and Urdu—educates workers on their legal rights, Wage Protection System (WPS) bank accounts, health and safety codes, heat exhaustion midday work bans (June 15 – Sept 15), and the MOHRE 24/7 dispute helpline (`600 590000`).\n- **ILOE Scheme:** Under Federal Law, all employees in the private and public sectors must enroll in the **Involuntary Loss of Employment (ILOE)** insurance scheme (`iloe.ae`). For employees with basic salaries under AED 16,000, the fee is just AED 5 per month (AED 60/year), providing up to 3 months of 60% salary compensation in the event of contractual termination.\n\n---\n\n### Stage 7: Electronic Labor Card & Digital Residence Stamping\nUpon passing medical fitness and completing Tawjeeh orientation:\n1. MOHRE issues your **Electronic Work Permit / Labor Card**, officially registering your corporate sponsorship, designated trade profession, and labor contract number.\n2. GDRFA / ICP finalizes your **2-Year Employment Residence Visa**.\n   - *Paperless Era:* Physical visa stickers inside passports have been completely phased out in favor of the next-generation **ICP Digital Identity Card**. Your Emirates ID now doubles as your residence visa, verified digitally by airlines and border control.\n\n---\n\n## 3.
