## 1. Saudi Arabia Work Visa Check Online (`visa.mofa.gov.sa`, `ksavisa.sa` & Qiwa)

Saudi Arabia is the #1 employment corridor for Indian tradespeople, drivers, welders, electricians, hospitality staff, and engineers. Because Saudi Arabia transitioned from the legacy **Enjaz (Enjazit)** portal to the unified **KSA Visa & MOFA Platform**, many workers get confused between their **E-Number**, **Wakala Block Number**, and **Stamped/Digital e-Visa**.

### Method A: Checking Saudi Visa on the Official MOFA Portal (`visa.mofa.gov.sa`)
1. Open the official Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs portal at **`https://visa.mofa.gov.sa`**.
2. Click the **"E"** language toggle in the top navigation bar to switch the interface from Arabic to **English** (if the page loads in Arabic).
3. Scroll down to the **"Enquiry"** or **"Track Your Application"** inquiry box on the homepage.
4. Choose your search criteria from the first dropdown:
   - Select **"Visa Application Number"** (if your agency gave you an **E-Number** starting with `E...`), OR select **"Visa Issuing Number / Invitation"** (if checking sponsor block details).
5. Enter your **Visa Application Number** in the first field.
6. Enter your exact **Passport Number** in uppercase letters (e.g., `Z4829104`) without spaces.
7. Enter the visual image **Captcha Security Code** shown on screen and click **"Search" / "Inquire"**.

### Method B: Using the Unified KSA Visa Platform (`ksavisa.sa`)
1. Visit **`https://ksavisa.sa`** (the official consolidated Saudi national visa ecosystem).
2. Click **"Track Application"** in the top menu bar.
3. Enter your **Application Reference Number** alongside your **Passport Number** and complete the security puzzle.
4. Click **Search** to inspect live VFS Tasheer / Embassy processing progress.

### How to Read Your Saudi Visa Result & Spot Fraud Immediately
When the MOFA database displays your record, inspect **four critical data points** line by line:

1. **Application Status**: Look for **"Visa Issued"** (or **"Passport Issued with Barcode"**). If it merely says **"Application Paid / Under Process"** (`Enjaz Receipt`), that document is **NOT a visa**—it is only a fee payment slip showing your passport was queued at Tasheer/VFS! Wait until the status changes to **Issued** with a scannable **Visa Number** before paying final deployment dues or booking flights.
2. **Profession / Occupation (*Mihna*)**: Translate or verify the Arabic profession printed on the visa barcode (e.g., *Electrician*, *Heavy Driver*, *Welder*, *Construction Worker*). Under Saudi Ministry of Human Resources (MHRSD) and [Takamol SVP Trade Test](/blogs/takamol-svp-skill-verification-program-saudi-arabia-trade-test-guide-2026) regulations, **your visa profession must match your trade qualification and Qiwa contract**.
3. **Sponsor Name (*Kafeel*)**: Confirm that the Saudi company name printed on the MOFA portal matches the exact employer name on your **Wakala** and signed employment offer letter.
4. **Embassy / Consulate Jurisdiction**: Check whether it is assigned to **Riyadh Mission – New Delhi** or **Mumbai Consulate**, matching your VFS Tasheer biometric submission region.

> [!NOTE]
> **Saudi Wakala & Qiwa Digital Contract Verification**: Before boarding your flight to Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, or [NEOM Megaprojects](/blogs/neom-project-latest-updates-mega-recruitment-2026-jobs-salary-qiwa-epc), ask your recruiter for your **Qiwa Digital Contract Offer** (`qiwa.sa`). Every genuine Saudi employer must issue an electronic labor contract on **Qiwa** specifying exact basic salary (SAR), housing/food allowance, 24-month contract tenure, and paid overtime before arrival. Explore our dedicated [Saudi Arabia Work Visa Hub](/countries/saudi-arabia) for full salary and labor law protections.

## 2. UAE & Dubai Work Visa Check Online (`gdrfad.gov.ae`, `smartservices.icp.gov.ae` & MOHRE)

Checking a United Arab Emirates (UAE) work visa is where candidates make the most common mistake. In the UAE, immigration records are split between **Dubai** and the **remaining 6 Emirates (Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, Umm Al Quwain)**. Checking a Dubai visa on the federal Abu Dhabi portal—or vice versa—returns **"No Record Found"**, causing unnecessary panic!

### Method A: If Your Employer Is in Dubai (GDRFA Dubai)
If your employment visa or entry permit is issued under any Dubai mainland company or Dubai Free Zone (JAFZA, DAFZA, DMCC, DSO, Dubai South), verify exclusively through the **General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA)**:
1. Visit the official portal at **`https://smart.gdrfad.gov.ae`** (or navigate via **`https://www.gdrfad.gov.ae`** -> *Services* -> *Visa Status Inquiry*).
2. Under **Search By**, choose **"Passport"** (to check purely via passport details) OR **"Application / File Number"** (using the entry permit file number printed at the top right of your UAE e-Visa copy).
3. If searching by **Passport**:
   - Enter your **Passport Number**.
   - Select **Nationality** (`India`).
   - Enter your **Date of Birth** (`DD/MM/YYYY`).
   - Check the Captcha box and click **Search**.
4. **Result Displayed**: A green confirmation badge will show your **Visa Status** (`Active`, `Used`, or `Issued`), **File Number**, **Visa Type**, and **Expiry Date**.

### Method B: For Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, RAK, Fujairah & UAQ (ICP Smart Services)
If your visa was issued anywhere in the UAE outside Dubai, verify it through the **Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP)**:
1. Open **`https://smartservices.icp.gov.ae/echannels/web/client/default.html#/fileValidity`**.
2. Toggle **"Search By"** to **Passport Information**.
3. Select **"Residency"** or **"Visa"** as the inquiry type.
4. Enter carefully:
   - **Passport Number**
   - **Passport Expiry Date** (crucial — a mismatch by even 1 day will fail lookup!)
   - **Nationality** (`India`)
5. Complete the `"I am not a robot"` verification and press **Search**.
6. **Result Displayed**: The federal ledger displays your **Unified Number (UID)**, **Visa Status (`ACTIVE`)**, **Issue Date**, and **Expiry Date**.

### Method C: Check UAE Offer Letter & Work Permit Status on MOHRE (`inquiry.mohre.gov.ae`)
Before a UAE residency stamp or entry permit is even issued, every private-sector employer on the UAE mainland must register a legally binding **MOHRE Job Offer Letter** and submit an **Employment Contract**:
1. Go to **`https://inquiry.mohre.gov.ae`** (or download the official **MOHRE UAE** app on Android/iOS).
2. Under **Choose Service**, select **"Application Status"** or **"Job Offer Status"** / **"Print Electronic Work Permit"**.
3. Enter your **Transaction Number (MB / ST number)** or **Offer Number** and **Passport Details** to verify whether your **Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE)** work permit has been approved, your exact basic salary in **AED**, and whether the company has any Wage Protection System (**WPS**) blocks.

> [!WARNING]
> **Beware the UAE "2-Month Visit Visa" Job Scam!** Scammers frequently send candidates a genuine, verifiable 60-day **Tourist / Visit Entry Permit** (`Entry Permit Type: Tourism / Visit Short Term`) and tell them it is a "Dubai Company Work Visa." When you scan the GDRFA/ICP QR code, the portal says *Valid*, tricking workers into flying to Dubai illegally. **How to spot it:** Look at the **Visa Type** line on your entry permit PDF and portal result. A genuine UAE work permit explicitly reads **"Employment Visa / Work Entry Permit"** and includes a **MOHRE Work Permit Number / Offer ID**—never "Tourism" or "Leisure." Read our full [Dubai Construction & Employment Visa Rules Guide](/blogs/dubai-construction-work-jobs-guide-2026-salaries-trades-visa-process-labor-laws-wps), [UAE Warehouse Jobs Guide](/blogs/uae-warehouse-jobs-complete-guide-dubai-abu-dhabi-salary-visa-freezone), and [UAE Hub](/countries/uae) before traveling.

## 3. Qatar Work Visa Check Online (`moi.gov.qa` & `qatarvisacenter.com`)

Qatar requires all Indian work visa applicants to undergo pre-departure medical, biometric capture, and digital contract signing inside India at an authorized **Qatar Visa Center (QVC)** before final entry approval.

### Method A: Qatar MOI Visa Inquiry & Printing (`moi.gov.qa`)
1. Visit the official Qatar Ministry of Interior portal at **`https://portal.moi.gov.qa/wps/portal/MOIInternet/services/inquiries/visaservices/visaandprinting`** (or navigate via **`https://www.moi.gov.qa`** -> *Inquiries* -> *Visa Services* -> *Visa Inquiry & Printing*).
2. Select the radio button **"By Passport Number"** (or choose *"By Visa Number"* if you already hold the 14-digit Qatar visa PDF).
3. Enter your **Passport Number** and select **Nationality** (`India`).
4. Enter the exact **Image Verification Code** (Captcha) and click **Submit**.

### Understanding Qatar MOI Status Codes
| Qatar MOI Portal Status | Exact Operational Meaning | Next Step for Candidate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ready to Print** | Visa is fully approved by Qatar MOI after QVC medical & biometric pass | Download official visa PDF & prepare for flight |
| **Valid / Used** | Visa is active or candidate has already entered Qatar | Complete QID card processing post-arrival |
| **Under Process / Pending Medical** | Sponsor applied for Visa Number; awaiting QVC completion | Attend scheduled [Qatar Visa Center (QVC)](/blogs/qatar-work-visa-qvc-process-guide-2026-medical-biometrics-contract-qid) appointment |
| **Rejected / Cancelled** | Application denied (medical unfit, security ban, or quota issue) | Contact sponsoring HR; do not pay flight costs |

### Method B: Qatar Visa Center (QVC) Pre-Departure Tracking (`qatarvisacenter.com`)
1. Open **`https://www.qatarvisacenter.com`** and select Country = **India**, Language = **English**.
2. Click **"Track Application"**.
3. Enter your **Passport Number**, **Visa Number**, and Captcha.
4. The QVC dashboard displays real-time granular statuses for:
   - Biometric Enrolment Status
   - Medical Screening Result (Fit / Unfit / Further Tests)
   - Employment Contract E-Signing Completion
   - Final MOI Visa Approval Release

## 6. Europe (Schengen), UK, Canada, Australia, Singapore, Japan & South Korea Verification

Verification procedures outside the Gulf differ substantially because Europe and developed Pacific nations enforce strict **GDPR privacy laws** and **paperless digital e-Visa ledgers**.

### 1. Australia (100% Paperless Digital Check via VEVO)
Australia does **not** issue paper visa stickers inside passports. If an agent sends you a photo of an "Australian Visa Sticker pasted on a passport," it is **100% FAKE**.
- **How to Verify Genuine Australian Visas**:
  1. Access the official Department of Home Affairs **VEVO (Visa Entitlement Verification Online)** portal at **`https://online.immi.gov.au/evo/firstParty?actionType=query`** (or install the **myVEVO** mobile app).
  2. Select Document Type: **Passport**.
  3. Choose Reference Type: **Transaction Reference Number (TRN)**, **Visa Grant Number**, or **Visa Evidence Number**.
  4. Enter your **Date of Birth**, **Passport Number**, and **Country of Passport (`India`)**.
  5. Submit to verify your active **Skills in Demand / Subclass 482** grant, exact expiry date, and legal work rights. See full details in our [Australia Skills in Demand (Subclass 482) Guide](/blogs/australia-skills-in-demand-visa-subclass-482-complete-guide-2026-jobs-salary-tra-pr).

### 2. Canada (IRCC Secure Account, Application Status Tracker & VFS)
Canada requires mandatory in-person biometrics at **VFS Global** in India; no Canadian work permit can ever be stamped without a VFS biometric receipt.
- **How to Verify Genuine Canadian Work Permits**:
  1. Log directly into the official **[IRCC Portal / GCKey Account](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/account.html)** or the **[IRCC Application Status Tracker](https://ircc-tracker-suivi.apps.cic.gc.ca)** using your **UCI (Unique Client Identifier)** and **Application Number** (starting with `W...` for Work Permits).
  2. Never trust a standalone PDF screenshot of a "Port of Entry Letter" without inspecting it directly inside a live `canada.ca` session.
  3. Track physical passport stamping courier progress at **`https://www.vfsglobal.com`** using your VFS Tracking Reference ID. Learn how to spot fake LMIA offers in our [Canada Work Permit & LMIA Guide](/blogs/canada-work-permit-lmia-process-guide-2026-tfwp-jobs-salary-pr) and [Canada Hub](/countries/canada).

### 3. Europe & Schengen Countries (Croatia, Poland, Germany, Malta, France, Bulgaria, Russia)
Because EU GDPR privacy rules prevent countries like Poland and Germany from displaying a stranger's personal details publicly by passport number alone, follow country-specific verification protocols:
- **Croatia (`crovisa.mvep.hr`)**: Enter your official **Application Serial Number** and **PIN** obtained at VFS Global Croatia to view live embassy decision progress. Work permit approvals (*Dozvola za boravak i rad*) can also be verified with the sponsoring company's local **MUP Policijska uprava** receipt. Explore [Croatia Jobs & Visa Hub](/countries/croatia).
- **Poland (Type D Work Visa & Voivodeship Permit)**: Genuine Polish work permits (*Zezwolenie na pracę*) are issued by a provincial **Urząd Wojewódzki** bearing stamped signatures or qualified electronic seals (*ePUAP*), followed by Type D embassy tracking via **e-Konsulat / VFS Global Poland**.
- **Malta (`identita.gov.mt`)**: Single Permit (*Still Waiting / Approval in Principle — AIP*) applications submitted by Maltese employers generate official **Identità Portal Tracking Numbers** before VFS stamping. Read our [Malta Single Permit Guide](/blogs/malta-work-visa-single-permit-complete-guide-2026-jobs-salary-identita-vfs).
- **Germany (Chancenkarte / Work Visa)**: Applications submitted via the **Consular Services Portal (`digital.diplo.de`)** and **VFS Global Germany** display real-time tracking in your own login dashboard alongside **ZAV Federal Employment Agency** pre-approvals. Explore [Germany Hub](/countries/germany) & [Germany Opportunity Card Guide](/blogs/germany-opportunity-card-chancenkarte-eu-blue-card-complete-guide-2026).
- **France, Bulgaria & Russia**: VFS Global India tracking covers France & Bulgaria Schengen/National D tracks (visit [France Hub](/countries/france) and [Bulgaria Hub](/countries/bulgaria)); **Russia Work Visas** mandate an authentic **MVD Yellow Invitation (*Priglashenie*)** verify-stamped before submitting to VFS/Russian Embassy—read our [Russia Work Visa Guide](/blogs/russia-work-visa-complete-guide-2026-jobs-salaries-mvd-invitation-process-safety).

### 4. Singapore, South Korea & Japan
- **Singapore (`mom.gov.sg`)**: Once an In-Principle Approval (**IPA**) letter is issued, verify your Work Permit or S Pass status on the official Ministry of Manpower **CheckWorkPass** service or scan the **SGWorkPass** QR barcode using your **FIN** and **Passport/Date of Birth**. Review our [Singapore Work Permit & S Pass Guide](/blogs/singapore-work-permit-s-pass-complete-guide-2026-jobs-salary-mom-ipa).
- **South Korea (`visa.go.kr`)**: Visit the **Korea Visa Portal** -> *Check Application Status* -> enter Passport Number, Name, and DOB to verify an official **CCVI (Confirmation of Visa Issuance)** or EPS E-9 grant. See [South Korea E-9 & E-7 Guide](/blogs/south-korea-work-visa-e9-eps-e7-skilled-complete-guide-2026-jobs-salary-topik).
- **Japan (`moj.go.jp` / VFS)**: Verify your original **Certificate of Eligibility (COE)** issued by Japan’s Immigration Services Agency (carrying electronic QR codes for digital COEs) prior to VFS Embassy submission. See [Japan SSW & TITP Guide](/blogs/japan-work-visa-ssw-titp-complete-guide-2026-jobs-salary-prometric-coe).

### 1. Check Wafid (GAMCA) Medical Report Online by Passport Number (`wafid.com`)
Before Gulf embassies stamp your visa (mandatory for Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman, and Bahrain), you must pass your **Wafid GCC Health Checkup**:
- Visit **`https://wafid.com`** -> Click **Medical Status** -> Select **"Check Your Medical Examination Result"**.
- Enter your **Passport Number** and select **Nationality (`India`)**.
- Instantly confirm whether the clinic uploaded your final result as **"FIT"** or **"UNFIT"**. Never hand over cash to an agent who claims they can "pass an Unfit medical with money." Check full medical guidelines in our [Wafid GAMCA Medical Complete Guide](/blogs/wafid-gamca-medical-examination-2026-online-appointment-rules-fit-unfit-criteria).

### 2. Verify Registered Recruiting Agent & Emigration Clearance on MEA `eMigrate` (`emigrate.gov.in`)
If your passport carries the **"Emigration Check Required" (ECR)** stamp (typically candidates who have not passed Class 10 matriculation), you **cannot legally board a flight** to any of the 18 notified countries (all GCC nations, Malaysia, etc.) without digital **Emigration Clearance (EC)** from the **Protector of Emigrants (PoE)**:
- Visit **`https://emigrate.gov.in`** -> **Emigrant / Recruiting Agent Services**.
- Under **"Search Registered RAs"**, type the agency’s **Registration Certificate (RC) Number** or company name to prove they hold an active license from the **Ministry of External Affairs (MEA)**, Government of India.
- Check your **EC Status** using your **Passport Number** and **Employment Contract (FE) Number** to guarantee your job contract, minimum wage, and **Pravasi Bharatiya Bima Yojana (PBBY)** insurance are officially logged with the Indian Government. Read our dedicated [Bihar & UP Gulf Work Visa Scam Protection Guide](/blogs/bihar-up-gulf-work-visa-guide-process-scam-protection-2026).

### 3. Verify Passport Seva PCC & Takamol Trade Certificates

## 8. Forensic Fake Visa Detection Checklist: 7 Red Flags That Expose Scams

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Can I check my Saudi Arabia work visa status online using ONLY my passport number?
**Answer:** On the official **`visa.mofa.gov.sa`** and **`ksavisa.sa`** portals, Saudi MOFA requires **two matching identifiers** for security: your **Passport Number** plus your **Visa Application Number (E-Number)** or Visa Number. If an agent refuses to share the `E-Number` printed on your Enjaz/MOFA application slip, treat that delay as a serious red flag and demand the slip immediately.

### Q2: Why does the UAE ICP website show "No Record Found" even though I have a valid Dubai visa PDF?
**Answer:** This is the most common false alarm for UAE workers! **Dubai** manages its own immigration system via **GDRFA Dubai (`gdrfad.gov.ae`)**, completely separate from the federal **ICP (`smartservices.icp.gov.ae`)** database used by Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, and the Northern Emirates. If your employer is licensed in Dubai, check **`gdrfad.gov.ae`** (by Passport Number + Nationality + Date of Birth), and your active record will appear immediately.

### Q3: How can I check if a UAE Dubai job offer letter is real before medical or visa stamping?
**Answer:** Every genuine mainland UAE job offer must be registered on the **Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE)** portal (`inquiry.mohre.gov.ae`). Ask your agency or employer for the **Transaction Number / Offer ID** (`MB` or `ST` reference) and verify your exact salary, job title, and employer trade license directly on the official MOHRE app or website.

### Q4: What does "Ready to Print" mean on the Qatar MOI visa inquiry website?
**Answer:** **"Ready to Print"** on `moi.gov.qa` is the final green signal confirming that your Qatar Visa Center (QVC) medical exam, digital biometrics, and e-contract passed all security clearances and the **Qatar Ministry of Interior has approved and issued your official Work Entry Visa**.

### Q5: How do I verify an Australian Subclass 482 / Skills in Demand visa without a passport sticker?
**Answer:** Australia abolished physical visa passport labels. Genuine Australian work visas are 100% digital and must be checked via the **Department of Home Affairs VEVO portal** (`online.immi.gov.au/evo`) using your **Passport Number, Date of Birth, and Visa Grant Number / TRN**.

### Q6: An agent sent me a Gulf e-Visa PDF with a QR code, and scanning the QR code shows my name in plain text. Is it original?
**Answer:** **Be very careful!** A legitimate Gulf government QR code resolves inside an **encrypted `.gov` web URL** (such as `https://visa.mofa.gov.sa/...` or `https://portal.moi.gov.qa/...`) or displays government-signed verification on the official portal. Anyone can generate a free plain-text QR code on Google containing your name and paste it onto a forged PDF. Always verify directly on the official government website manually.

### Q7: Can a company convert a Dubai Visit/Tourist Visa into an Employment Work Visa after I land in the UAE?
**Answer:** While status adjustment inside the UAE exists legally under specific MOHRE procedures, **"Come on Visit Visa, we will give you a job later" is the single biggest overseas recruitment trap in India**. Entering the UAE on a tourist visa for guaranteed work leaves you completely unprotected under Indian **eMigrate** rules, strips your repatriation protections, and frequently ends with workers stranded illegally on overstay fines. Always travel on a verified **Employment Entry Permit** issued prior to departure.

### Q8: How do I check if my Saudi Arabia Takamol trade test result has updated on my visa profile?
**Answer:** Once you complete your practical and theory exam at an authorized Indian SVP center, check your pass/fail status directly on **`svp-international.com`** using your Passport Number and Date of Birth. Your certificate automatically links to Saudi **Qiwa** and **MOFA Enjaz** systems within 24–72 hours. View our complete guide on [Takamol SVP Result & Certificate Check Online](/blogs/takamol-svp-skill-verification-program-saudi-arabia-trade-test-guide-2026).

### Q9: What should I do if the profession written on my Saudi or Kuwait visa is different from the job I interviewed for?
**Answer:** Stop immediately and **do not proceed with final stamping or flight boarding** until clarified in writing. For example, if you passed an interview for *Industrial Electrician (SAR 2,200)*, but the Saudi MOFA portal displays *"Building Labor / Worker"*, your actual salary, Qiwa contract category, Iqama renewal eligibility, and driver's license rights inside Saudi Arabia will be legally locked to the lower *Labor* category.

### Q10: How can I verify a Europe (Poland / Croatia / Malta) work permit from India?
**Answer:** For Croatia, track the embassy visa directly on **`crovisa.mvep.hr`** with your Serial Number and PIN. For Malta, request your **Identità Single Permit Application Reference** (`identita.gov.mt`). For Poland and Germany, confirm your original Voivodeship Permit / ZAV pre-approval via your employer and verify biometric appointment updates directly on **VFS Global India** (`vfsglobal.com`) or **`digital.diplo.de`**.

### Q11: Can I verify my Wafid (GAMCA) medical fitness online without going back to the clinic?
**Answer:** Yes. Within **48 to 72 hours** of your examination, go to **`https://wafid.com`**, open **View Medical Reports**, and enter your **Passport Number** and **Nationality**. Your official status will display directly as **Fit** or **Unfit**.

### Q12: Why does an ECR passport require extra clearance before flying on a Gulf work visa?
**Answer:** Under the Indian Emigration Act 1983, workers holding **ECR (Emigration Check Required)** passports (non-matriculates) traveling to notified GCC/Asian countries for employment must obtain digital **Emigration Clearance (EC)** on **`emigrate.gov.in`**, guaranteeing an attested contract, minimum wage protection, and **Pravasi Bharatiya Bima Yojana (PBBY)** insurance coverage. Only **MEA-registered Recruiting Agents (RAs)** can process ECR work deployments legally.

### Q13: How do I check my Saudi Arabia Wakala status online before VFS Tasheer stamping?
**Answer:** You can verify whether your employer has issued a legal **Saudi Wakala** via your recruiting agency's official **Enjaz / KSA Visa agency portal** using the **Visa Authorization Number** (`Visa No.`) and **Sponsor ID**. Make sure your passport details and agreed profession appear identically on both the Wakala and your Qiwa contract prior to passport submission.

### Q14: Is an employer's signed UAE MOHRE Offer Letter enough to confirm a Dubai employment visa?
**Answer:** No, a MOHRE Offer Letter (`MB/ST` transaction) is only **Stage 1** of UAE hiring. Verify the offer letter on **`inquiry.mohre.gov.ae`** first, complete your signature, and then wait for your official **Employment Entry Permit PDF** (verified via **`gdrfad.gov.ae`** for Dubai or **`smartservices.icp.gov.ae`** for other Emirates) before booking any international air ticket.

### Q15: How many days does it take for a newly issued Gulf or European visa to appear online?
**Answer:** Electronic visas for **UAE (ICP/GDRFA)**, **Qatar MOI**, **Oman ROP**, **Bahrain LMRA**, and **Australia VEVO** appear **immediately (within minutes of ministerial issuance)**. **Saudi MOFA (`visa.mofa.gov.sa`)** usually updates within **24–72 hours** after VFS Tasheer passport submission and embassy stamping. **European Schengen / Croatia (`crovisa.mvep.hr`)** tracking updates live inside VFS/embassy dispatch cycles, typically **10–21 working days** post-biometrics.
