## 4. Examination Pattern, Scoring Matrix & Passing Marks

The Takamol SVP examination is structured as a **rigorous two-tier assessment model** designed to evaluate both theoretical comprehension and hands-on operational craftsmanship under strict standardized conditions.

```
       [ TAKAMOL EVALUATION ARCHITECTURE ]
                         |
      +------------------+------------------+
      |                                     |
      v                                     v
STAGE 1: COMPUTER THEORY              STAGE 2: WORKSHOP PRACTICAL
- 30% Aggregate Weightage             - 70% Aggregate Weightage
- 20 to 30 Multiple Choice (MCQs)     - 90 to 180 Minutes Live Testing
- Hindi, Urdu, English, Tagalog       - Conducted in Accredited Lab
- Basic Safety, Symbols, Tool ID      - Evaluated on Workmanship & Speed
      |                                     |
      +------------------+------------------+
                         |
                         v
              [ PASSING BENCHMARK ]
         Theory: Minimum 50% Marks
         Practical: Minimum 50% - 60% Marks
         Aggregate: Successful Certification
```

### Stage 1: Computer-Based Theoretical Examination (30% Weightage)

The theoretical component evaluates basic cognitive competence, occupational safety literacy, and trade terminology:

- **Format**: 20 to 30 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) administered on a secure touch-screen computer terminal or tablet at the test center.
- **Multilingual Support**: Questions are displayed with multilingual audio-visual support in **Hindi, Urdu, English, Arabic, Bengali, and Tagalog**. Candidates do not need advanced English fluency; they can comfortably take the test in their native tongue.
- **Duration**: 30 to 45 minutes.
- **Core Subject Domains**:
  1. Occupational Health, Safety & Environment (**WHS/OSHA**) standards (fire prevention, hazard symbols, electrical isolation).
  2. Personal Protective Equipment (**PPE**) selection and correct usage.
  3. Reading engineering schematics, single-line diagrams (SLD), and trade blueprints.
  4. Unit conversions (millimeters to inches, bar to PSI, volts to kilowatts).
  5. Hand and power tool identification, preventative maintenance, and workshop etiquette.

### Stage 2: Practical Workshop Performance Assessment (70% Weightage)

The practical component represents the decisive heart of the Takamol examination. Candidates are placed in a fully equipped industrial testing bay matching Saudi workplace environments:

- **Duration**: 90 to 180 minutes depending on trade complexity.
- **Monitoring**: All testing bays are monitored via live high-definition video recording directly connected to the TVTC Quality Audit Directorate in Riyadh.
- **Evaluation Rubric**:

| Assessment Criterion                 | Weightage | What the Evaluator Measures                                                                                                                                          |
| :----------------------------------- | :-------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Safety Compliance & PPE**          | **15%**   | Proper donning of safety helmet, steel-toe boots, eye protection, earplugs, welding apron; conducting visual safety checks before powering equipment.                |
| **Tool Selection & Handling**        | **20%**   | Selecting the exact tool for the designated task (e.g., using a torque wrench instead of an adjustable spanner; correct electrode angle; proper pipe cutter sizing). |
| **Execution Accuracy to Blueprint**  | **40%**   | Following given blueprint dimensions within permissible tolerances (±1 mm in carpentry/machining; correct circuit wiring; pressure test hold).                       |
| **Workmanship, Finishing & Cleanup** | **15%**   | Neatness of wiring looms, absence of weld spatter/undercut, deburring pipe edges, proper cleanup of workshop bay after task completion.                              |
| **Oral Viva & Diagnostic Q&A**       | **10%**   | Responding to 2-3 direct diagnostic questions posed by the examiner regarding troubleshooting or equipment failure modes.                                            |

### Statutory Passing Marks

To be awarded a Takamol Certificate of Professional Competence, a candidate must:

1. Score at least **50% in the Theoretical Examination** (e.g., at least 15 correct answers out of 30).
2. Score at least **50% to 60% in the Practical Examination** (depending on whether the trade involves life-critical safety regulations, such as gas welding or high-voltage electrical installations).
3. Achieve an overall aggregate passing score. If a candidate passes theory but fails practical, the entire result is deemed **"Failed"**, requiring a practical re-examination.

### Trade 1: Building Electrician (كهربائي تمديدات مباني)

- **Theoretical Core**: Ohm’s Law ($V = I \times R$), power formula ($P = V \times I$), functions of Miniature Circuit Breakers (MCB) and Earth Leakage Circuit Breakers (ELCB/RCCB), wire gauge sizing according to load, color-coding conventions (Red/Yellow/Blue for live phases, Black/Blue for neutral, Green/Yellow for ground), IP ingress ratings for outdoor enclosures.
- **Practical Bench Tasks**:
  1. _Task 1_: Mark, cut, and bend a 20mm PVC conduit using a bending spring to create a 90-degree bend and saddle offset.
  2. _Task 2_: Wire a dual two-way staircase lighting circuit controlled from two independent switches, incorporating an intermediate switch.
  3. _Task 3_: Terminate a sub-distribution board (DB) with incoming ELCB and outgoing 10A/16A MCBs; perform insulation resistance testing using a 500V Megger.

- **Theoretical Core**: Understanding welding polarities (Direct Current Electrode Positive - DCEP vs DCEN), classification of electrodes under AWS standards (e.g., E6010 for deep penetration root pass, E7018 for low-hydrogen high-tensile fill passes), shielding gas selection (100% Argon for TIG vs 80/20 Argon/$CO_2$ mix for MIG), preheat and interpass temperature controls, identification of weld defects (undercut, porosity, incomplete fusion, excessive root penetration).
- **Practical Bench Tasks**:
  1. _Structural Welder_: Perform a single-V groove butt weld on 10mm carbon steel plates in the **3G (Vertical-Up)** position using AWS E7018 electrodes. Plate must undergo visual inspection and a 180-degree hydraulic root-bend test.
  2. _Pipe Welder_: Fit-up, tack, and complete a full-penetration weld on a 4-inch or 6-inch Schedule 40 carbon steel pipe in the **6G inclined (45-degree)** fixed position. Root pass executed with GTAW (TIG ER70S-6) followed by SMAW E7018 hot, fill, and cap passes.

**Answer**: Once your status on `svp-international.com` displays **"Passed"**, a green button labeled **"Download Certificate"** or **"Print Certificate"** will appear beside your score summary. ### Q3: What are the passing marks for the Takamol SVP examination?

**Answer**: Candidates must score a minimum of **50% in the Computer Theoretical Examination** (Stage 1) and between **50% and 60% in the Practical Workshop Examination** (Stage 2). Both components must be passed independently; high marks in theory cannot compensate for a failing score in practical craftsmanship.
