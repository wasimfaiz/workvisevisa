## Legal Framework: The Georgia D1 Immigration Visa & Work Residence Permit

Under Georgian immigration law, legal foreign employment is structured into a mandatory **two-tier legal framework**:

### 1. Tier 1: The National Long-Stay Immigration Visa (Category D1)

- **Governing Law:** Law of Georgia on the Legal Status of Aliens and Stateless Persons, Article 8.
- **Purpose:** Specifically issued to third-country nationals (including Indian citizens) traveling to Georgia for the purpose of contractual employment or entrepreneurial activity.
- **Statutory Validity:** Issued for up to 90 days or 180 days with multiple entries.
- **Issuing Authority:** Consular Department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Georgia via the **Embassy of Georgia in New Delhi**.
- **Crucial Rule:** You **cannot** start working legally in Georgia on a tourist visa (Category C) or electronic tourist visa. Attempting to work on a tourist visa results in immediate employer fines, permit revocation, and administrative deportation.

### 2. Tier 2: The Work Residence Permit (_შრომითი ბინადრობის ნებართვა_)

- **Governing Body:** Public Service Development Agency (SDA) under the Ministry of Justice, processed at the **Public Service Hall (_Justiciis Sakhli_)**.
- **Application Window:** Submitted in Georgia after arrival on a valid D1 visa, at least 40 calendar days prior to the expiration of the lawful stay duration.
- **Key Statutory Threshold:** The sponsoring employer must demonstrate an annual turnover of at least **GEL 50,000 per foreign employee** (or GEL 35,000 in educational/medical sectors), and the employee's monthly salary must not fall below five times the national subsistence minimum.
- **Validity & Renewal:** Granted for an initial period of **6 months to 1 year**, renewable annually up to a maximum of 5 to 6 years, after which the foreign worker qualifies for Permanent Residence.

## Critical Anti-Scam Advisory: 6 Red Flags of Georgian Visa Fraud

Because Georgia has strict border controls and high electronic scrutiny, fraudulent agents have created dangerous recruitment traps. Protect yourself by recognizing these 6 red flags:

### 1. The "Tourist e-Visa to Work Permit" Trap (Airport Deportation Racket)

**The Trap:** An agent charges ₹1.5 Lakh to ₹3 Lakh, hands you an electronic tourist e-Visa PDF downloaded from `evisa.gov.ge`, and says: _"Bhaiya, Georgia jao, wahan company mein kaam shuru ho jayega aur visa change ho jayega."_
**The Ground Reality:** **100% FRAUD AND A GUARANTEED DEPORTATION.** Georgian border police at Tbilisi and Batumi airports subject Indian tourists to intense screening. If you carry work clothes, welding tools, or cannot prove hotel bookings and personal funds of $1,500+ USD, you will be denied entry, placed in an airport holding cell, and deported on the next return flight at your own cost. Furthermore, Georgian law strictly prohibits changing a tourist visa to a work residence permit from within the country.

### 2. Counterfeit Public Service Hall / SDA Approvals

**The Trap:** Scammers show you a photo of a document with the Georgian national emblem (St. George slaying the dragon) claiming to be an "Advance Work Permit."
**The Ground Reality:** The Public Service Hall (**Justiciis Sakhli**) does not issue work residence permits to candidates who are still in India. Residence permits are applied for **in person in Georgia** after you have arrived on an authentic D1 immigration visa and completed physical biometrics at the service hall.

### 3. Fictitious Companies Not Registered with NAPR

Before paying any advance for document translation, obtain the sponsoring employer's 9-digit **Identification Code (_საიდენტიფიკაციო კოდი_)**.
Visit the official **National Agency of Public Registry portal ([napr.gov.ge](https://napr.gov.ge))** and enter the code. If the company is listed as terminated, suspended, or nonexistent, the job offer is completely fabricated.

### 4. Fake Promises of "Walk to Schengen / Europe"

Scammers falsely market Georgia as part of the Schengen Area, promising that "after 6 months in Georgia, you can cross into Germany or France." Georgia is a sovereign Caucasus nation located hundreds of kilometers away from the Schengen border. Any agent promising illegal border crossings is leading you into dangerous human trafficking traps.

### 5. Outrageously Inflated Salary Claims (€3,500/Month)

If an agent offers an unskilled packaging, warehouse, or farming job in Georgia paying **€3,000 EUR (₹2.7 Lakh/month)** with "free flights and no interview," you are dealing with advance-fee fraud. Genuine Georgian industrial wages range from **1,300 to 3,400 GEL (₹40,000 to ₹85,000+ INR)**.

### 6. Cloned Visa Verification Portals

Fraudsters construct fake websites with names like `georgia-visa-online.org` or `tbilisi-immigration-gov.com` that show a fake "Approved" status when your passport number is entered. Real visa tracking is conducted strictly on **[geoconsul.gov.ge](https://geoconsul.gov.ge)** or through authorized VFS tracking. - PSK PCC apostille & certified Georgian translation: 10–12 days

### Q3: What is the difference between a D1 Visa and a Work Residence Permit?

- **Category D1 Visa:** A multi-entry immigration visa sticker affixed to your passport by the Georgian Embassy in New Delhi, allowing you to travel to Georgia specifically for employment.
- **Work Residence Permit (_შრომითი ბინადრობის მოწმობა_):** A biometric plastic ID card issued by the Public Service Hall in Georgia after your arrival, granting you legal resident status and employment rights for up to 1 year (renewable).

### Q4: Is English or Georgian language proficiency mandatory for the visa?

**No.** For construction trades, heavy equipment operation, welding, and agricultural labor, there is no requirement to pass IELTS or Georgian language examinations. Basic conversational English or the ability to understand international worksite safety commands is beneficial, and bilingual supervisors coordinate daily tasks.

### Q5: Can I switch employers in Georgia after arriving?

Yes, under Georgian labor regulations, a worker can switch employers provided proper administrative procedures are followed. You cannot work informally without registration. Your new employer must execute a new labor contract and apply for an amendment of your Work Residence Permit through the Public Service Hall.

### Q6: What healthcare coverage do Indian workers receive in Georgia?

All legal workers holding an employment contract must maintain corporate health insurance. Sponsoring employers provide private or group healthcare coverage covering emergency medical treatment, hospitalizations, surgical interventions, and work-related injuries across licensed Georgian clinics.

### Q7: Can my family (spouse and children) join me in Georgia?

Yes. Once you have received your Work Residence Permit, maintain continuous lawful employment, and demonstrate adequate accommodation and income to support dependants, you can sponsor your spouse and minor children for a **D4 Family Reunification Visa** and subsequent residence permits.

### Q8: Can I convert my Indian driving license to a Georgian driving license?

Yes. India and Georgia are signatories to the 1968 Vienna Convention on Road Traffic. Workers with a valid Indian Smart Card Driving License and an International Driving Permit (IDP) can drive legally during their initial months. Following the issuance of your Work Residence Permit, you can submit your apostilled Indian driving license and medical fitness certificate to the Service Agency of the Ministry of Internal Affairs (**MIA Service Agency**) for conversion.

Verified Georgian corporate employers provide **free company-managed housing**. ### Q10: Do I need an MEA Apostille on my Indian Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)?

**Yes, strictly mandatory.** Georgia is a contracting state to the Hague Apostille Convention of 5 October 1961. Plain notary attestations will cause immediate visa rejection.
